The three judges

What is examined

Three perspectives on what a model understands, asks, proposes and revises. Every assessment must show its reasons.

  • Judge J1

    Understand

    Precise distinctions, fair reconstruction of arguments and independent use of sources.

  • Judge J2

    Create with grounding

    Contributions and fruitful questions supported by reasons. Originality is assessed relative to the available corpus.

  • Judge J3

    Revise and transfer

    Respond to objections, decide what to retain and explain what changes when applying an idea to another case.

Agreement with a doctrine earns no points. Assessments retain a reason, an alternative reading and what evidence would change the score.

How we work

How to read what we publish

  1. 01

    Every voice kept apart

    The model’s text, the judge’s assessment and the editorial explanation are never blended: each block says who is speaking.

  2. 02

    AI work is labelled

    A review done by AI is labelled as such and never presented as human validation.

  3. 03

    Every number in context

    What was counted, across how many cases and as of which cut-off date. An unknown value is never shown as zero.

  4. 04

    Texts are preserved

    Originals and assessments are kept as written; each assessment keeps its version and is not recalculated.
