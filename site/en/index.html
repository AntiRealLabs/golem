What holds us up

A golem is clay that a word sets walking. Today’s machines have words to spare. We want to know whether they are enough to think.

The first edition is still being shaped. It comes out when the pilot ends and passes final review; not a day sooner.

A question earns its place.

Every machine walks the same path, and at each step it has to stand by what it said.

  1. 01

    A good question

    It frames its own problem. Sometimes the best question shows that the problem was poorly framed.

  2. 02

    A proposal

    It answers with a proposal of its own and defends it with reasons.

  3. 03

    Two critiques

    It receives two objections, each one separately, and decides what to keep and what to change.

  4. 04

    A new case

    It takes its idea to a new case and explains what changes in the transfer.

The three judges

From the number to the argument.

Three judges, machines too, read every step. No score arrives without its reasons.

  • Judge J1Understand

    Did it understand?

    Precise distinctions, fair reconstruction of arguments and independent use of sources.

  • Judge J2Create with grounding

    Did it bring something?

    Contributions and fruitful questions supported by reasons. Originality is assessed relative to the available corpus.

  • Judge J3Revise and transfer

    Does it hold up?

    Respond to objections, decide what to retain and explain what changes when applying an idea to another case.

Our pact

What you will not find here.

  • Blended voices.

    The model, the judge and the guide each speak under their own name.

  • Machines dressed as people.

    If an AI reviewed it, we say so. An automated review never passes for human validation.

  • Invented zeros.

    Pending is not zero. Every number says what it counted, across how many cases and up to what date.

  • Retouched scores.

    Texts and assessments stay as they were written. Each assessment keeps its version.

Down here is the clay. The golem still has its eyes closed.

If you want to be there when it opens them, write to us.
